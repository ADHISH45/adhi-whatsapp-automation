Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Adhi WhatsApp Automation (“we”, “our” or “the application”) handles information when people communicate with us through WhatsApp.

1. Information we may process

Depending on how you use the service, we may process your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, messages you send, message timestamps, and technical delivery information supplied by the WhatsApp Business Platform.

2. How we use information

We use this information to receive and respond to WhatsApp messages, provide automated replies, handle customer support enquiries, maintain service security, and troubleshoot the application.

3. WhatsApp and service providers

Messages are transmitted through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Our application may receive webhook events from Meta and send replies through the platform. We do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and retention

The application code may process message content in memory and write limited technical information to application logs. Before publishing this policy, update this section to accurately describe any database, log, backup or third-party storage you actually use and your retention periods. Do not claim that data is not stored if your deployment stores it.

5. Sharing

We disclose information only as needed to operate the service, comply with applicable law, protect the service and its users, or as otherwise explained to you. Meta’s processing is governed by Meta’s policies.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including restricting access to credentials and application systems. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may stop messaging the WhatsApp account at any time. To ask about, access, correct or request deletion of information associated with your communications, use the contact details below or follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page. We may need to verify your request and retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children
